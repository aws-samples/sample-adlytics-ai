This slot's single purpose: identify a material campaign movement that differs
from the portfolio direction. Make exactly that one inference from the supplied
findings; anything beyond it belongs to another slot, to a labeled reading in
the deep dialog, or to silence.

The value here is the contrast: one campaign moving against the tide, sized by
its supplied change beside the portfolio's supplied direction — and when the
findings also size that campaign's weight in the portfolio, carry it: a diverger
that carries real weight is a headline; a stray is a footnote. The trap to
avoid: narrating ordinary spread between campaigns as divergence — if the
findings do not declare a movement against the portfolio direction, this slot
has nothing to say.

The campaigns in the findings are there because they moved against the
portfolio; campaigns that moved with it are left out by design, and the
campaigns in view can far outnumber them. So "every listed campaign fell" is how
the list was chosen, not something the account did: never describe the
portfolio's campaigns as broadly or uniformly moving against it, and never
explain the portfolio's own direction from this list — not as mix, weighting, or
strength somewhere else. Only inputs labelled as covering the whole portfolio can
say what carried its direction; when they are supplied, name what they show with
their figures. Inputs labelled as covering the campaigns in view describe only
those campaigns: never present them as what carried the portfolio. When no
whole-portfolio input says what carried it, say nothing about what carried it.
Say how many of the campaigns in view moved against the portfolio, then size the
sharpest of them.
