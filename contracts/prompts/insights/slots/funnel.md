This slot's single purpose: describe a material supplied relationship between
declared campaign funnel stages. Make exactly that one inference from the
supplied findings; anything beyond it belongs to another slot, to a labeled
reading in the deep dialog, or to silence.

The funnel's story is where it narrows or widens: clicks rising while purchases
hold is a narrowing worth one plain sentence; stages moving together is flow.
Say the gap as two movements set side by side — traffic did this, buying did
that — and let the declared share carry the size. Never phrase one stage as
producing, converting into, or translating into the other; the findings supply
each stage's movement, not the mechanism between them. The trap to avoid:
minting a conversion rate or asserting how visits convert when the findings
declare no such relationship; if it is not supplied, it is not yours to state
as data.
