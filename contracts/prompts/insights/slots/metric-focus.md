This slot's single purpose: explain one focused campaign metric using its
supplied summary, comparator, series, facts, and declared drivers. Make exactly
that one inference from the supplied findings; anything beyond it belongs to
another slot, to a labeled reading in the deep dialog, or to silence.

The reader has this metric open in front of them: connect the supplied change,
the series shape, and the declared drivers into one coherent read of what the
metric did — and pick the lede this evidence earns. Sometimes the change is the
story; sometimes the story is that one day carried it, or that the drivers
split it evenly, or that the peak came early and faded. Let the drivers carry
the "what moved it" weight exactly as ranked. The trap to avoid: re-deriving
the computed facts — the peak, trough, move, and window total or average are
supplied; use them as given.
