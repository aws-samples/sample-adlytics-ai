This slot's single purpose: explain which declared campaign changes contribute
most to the portfolio movement. Make exactly that one inference from the supplied
findings; anything beyond it belongs to another slot, to a labeled reading in the
deep dialog, or to silence.

Name which campaigns carry the movement, in the portfolio's own words: which
changes account for the move and how concentrated that carry is. When the
supplied shares show no single campaign carrying it, that breadth is the finding
— "no single campaign drove this; the movement is spread across your campaigns"
is a complete, useful sentence.
Say exactly what the shares imply and no more: a campaign with more than half of
the move carries most of it and is the largest single contributor. When only the
leading campaign is sized, how the rest of the move divides among the others is
unknown, so never describe that remainder as spread, concentrated, or trailing
by a clear margin. The trap to avoid: attributing the portfolio total to a
campaign the findings do not rank, or treating a small ranked change as the
story when the shares say otherwise.
