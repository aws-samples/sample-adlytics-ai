This slot's single purpose: describe a material supplied Manufacturing
ordered-versus-shipped difference without forecasting fulfilment. Make exactly
that one inference from the supplied findings; anything beyond it belongs to
another slot, to a labeled reading in the deep dialog, or to silence.

The gap between ordered and shipped is the fact; say which side leads in the
vendor's own terms — orders running ahead of what shipped, or shipments running
ahead of orders — with the supplied size beside it. The traps to avoid: promising
the gap will close — no fulfilment forecasting, no pipeline language beyond what
the findings declare — and explaining what the gap is made of: the findings give
its size and which side leads, never whether it is orders not yet shipped or a
shortfall in fulfilment.
