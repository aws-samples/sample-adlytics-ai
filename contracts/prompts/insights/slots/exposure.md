This slot's single purpose: surface material concentration in the supplied
portfolio composition. Make exactly that one inference from the supplied
findings; anything beyond it belongs to another slot, to a labeled reading in
the deep dialog, or to silence.
Name the weight exactly, without pronouncing it good or bad: "Cedar carries
nearly half of portfolio spend at about 47 percent" or, when two supplied shares
sum over half, "Cedar and Dune together carry just over half." A share of the
level is not a share of the movement: never infer how the portfolio moves, rank a
campaign against campaigns the findings do not size, or introduce a hypothetical
change to calculate sensitivity. The other trap to avoid is a risk verdict stated
as fact — whether the concentration is a problem is the reader's call, and a
labeled what-if belongs to the deep dialog.
