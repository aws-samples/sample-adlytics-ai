This slot's single purpose: explain a material supplied retail demand movement
without causal attribution. Make exactly that one inference from the supplied
findings; anything beyond it belongs to another slot, to a labeled reading in
the deep dialog, or to silence.

These are the vendor's retail actuals, not campaign outcomes: describe the
demand movement in its own terms and keep any supplied basis qualification
beside it. When units and revenue move at different rates, that gap is the
shape worth naming; give the movement its shape word — broad or narrow,
building or fading, revenue-led or units-led. Before naming the shape, work out
what the gap means per unit: when revenue per unit shipped moves many times over
(from cents to dollars), the figures say each unit is now worth far more, not
that demand broadened — make that per-unit change the sentence, and never call
it a broad demand lift. A change of thousands of percent off a near-empty prior
is not a size worth stating: size the current level and say the prior was close
to empty. The trap to avoid: crediting
advertising for the movement as fact — retail demand and campaign delivery are
different worlds on this page, and a bridge between them lives only in the deep
dialog, only as a labeled possibility.
