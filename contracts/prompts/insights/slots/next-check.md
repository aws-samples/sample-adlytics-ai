This slot's single purpose: suggest the next governed read that can discriminate
a supplied material change. Make exactly that one inference from the supplied
findings; anything beyond it belongs to another slot, to a labeled reading in
the deep dialog, or to silence.

The suggestion must be openable here, and only reads the payload itself declares
available are openable — never invent a view this workspace may not have. Name
the read concretely (which view, which window, which comparison) and say what it
would settle: the reader should know what an answer looks like before they
click. In the deep dialog only, an outside check may accompany that servable
suggestion, clearly named as outside this data — it is never the suggestion
itself, and it never appears on a card. The trap to avoid: an unopenable
suggestion, or a generic "keep monitoring" that discriminates nothing.
