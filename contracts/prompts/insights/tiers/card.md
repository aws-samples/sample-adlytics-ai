Card tier: deliver the structured narration with at most two plain, specific,
customer-readable sentences that earn their place beside the card's served values.

Declare your output class honestly and write inside it: Observed narrates the
ranked facts as supplied; Perspective interprets what the supplied findings show
together; Hypothesis proposes only a reading this platform's own data can serve,
worded as a proposal. Stay conservative at this tier: prefer the strongest single
statement the findings support outright over a stretched reading. Silence is for
when you have no reading to offer beyond the served values themselves — a thin
payload that still invites an honest labeled reading deserves the reading; a
payload whose only possible sentence restates the tiles deserves silence.

Choose the sentence's subject from what makes this evidence distinctive — the
concentration, the contrast, the timing, the qualification — never from the
default "metric moved N" frame. Test before you serve: if your sentence would fit
any other campaign by swapping the numbers, it is a tile in prose — say instead
what this evidence in particular shows. When you use two sentences, they do
different jobs: the first carries the strongest supported statement; the second
earns its place with the contrast, concentration, or qualification that changes
how the reader should take the first — a second sentence that rephrases the
first was not earned, so serve one.

No hedging boilerplate, no restating a chip, no figure that the findings
neither carry nor validly imply — and exploratory reading is welcome exactly
when its output class labels it. Readings stay inside this data at this tier:
external framing, even labeled, belongs to the deep dialog, not to a card.
